You are trying to use an engine built for one job to solve a problem that needs another.

CNP treats the mind as a set of competing energy centers, each with its own fuel and its own output. When the input does not match the output, the engine stalls, and that stall feels like procrastination, people-pleasing, anger or worry.

Naming which engine is running, and what it needs, is the first step to working with it instead of against it.

The framework

Four engines of action

Each archetype comes with a core conflict, a deep analysis, strengths and risks, real-life cases and a step-by-step roadmap.

Input vs. Output

The Creator

For those caught in analysis paralysis and collecting more than they ever make.

Performance vs. Connection

The Seller

For those who feel unseen, lonely, or like an impostor despite their charisma.

Complaint vs. Reform

The Warrior

For those filled with anger at inefficiency and injustice who need a way to channel it.

Anxiety vs. Planning

The Strategist

For those paralyzed by fear of future catastrophes and looking for control through preparation.
